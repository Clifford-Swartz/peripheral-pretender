# TC (U2249) on the PIC32CM1216MC00048: waveform outputs and capture

Research notes for the twin, for what the first round (counting, commands, buffers, events;
`tcprobe.c`, `tc.c`) left out: the waveform outputs on pins and capture. Sources, most
authoritative first: silicon (section 5), the data sheet (DS60001638E chapter 33), the DFP
headers, the twin's code (`hw/timer/mchp_tc.c`).

## 1. Setup on the desk board

| | |
|---|---|
| Outputs | TC3 WO0/WO1 on PB22/PB23 (function E, Arduino D6/D9), read back through PORT.IN with PINCFG.INEN |
| Stimulus | PB09 (A1, EXTINT9, function A): its pull resistor, turned by PORT.OUT, makes the pulses; EIC HIGH sense, ASYNCH, event out; EVSYS channel 0 asynchronous to TC3's event user (26) |
| Clock | GCLK_TC 48 MHz; COUNT8/16 with DIV64: a tick is 4/3 us |
| Tools | `firmware/hello/src/scope.h` (edge recorder in RAM, SysTick over 24 bits) |

## 2. Data sheet (33.6.2.6.1, 33.6.2.8, 33.6.3.2)

- NFRQ toggles WO[x] on each CCx match; MFRQ toggles WO[0] at each update (TOP = CC0); NPWM sets
  at the start or TOP and clears at the CCx match counting up (the other way down); MPWM is NPWM
  with TOP = CC0 and a one-tick negative pulse on WO[0] at each overflow. DRVCTRL.INVENx inverts.
- One-shot: at the end the waveform outputs are set to zero.
- Capture (CAPTENx) from the event input (TCEI, an asynchronous channel) or from the pin (COPENx).
  Event actions OFF/RETRIGGER/COUNT/START capture COUNT on the event; STAMP likewise and MAX at an
  overflow; PPW captures the period in CC0 and the pulse width in CC1 and restarts the counter on
  one edge; PWP swaps them; PW captures the width on the falling edge, clears and stops, and
  restarts on the rising edge. TCINV (or INVENx for pins) selects the edge.
- CCx and CCBUFx act as a FIFO: reading CCx moves CCBUFx up; a capture while MCx is still set is
  lost and sets ERR.
- CAPTMIN/CAPTMAX (CTRLA.CAPTMODEn) are described, but the MC00's DFP has no CAPTMODE field.

## 4. Answers from silicon (2026-10-21)

1. **Writable bits**: CTRLA 0x00338FFC: no CAPTMODE (bits 24–28 do not stick), and **bit 15
   writable although the data sheet has no field there** (the JH01's ATDF has none either).
   DRVCTRL 0x03.
2. **Waveforms** (W1–W9) follow the data sheet, with these details:
   - The outputs start about one tick after the enable write (2 us at DIV64, under 1 us at DIV1).
     Before that, and while disabled, the pins read 0, INVEN or not.
   - NPWM: high for CCx ticks counting up; counting down, set when the counter reaches CCx and
     cleared at the reload (high CCx + 1 ticks). CC0 = 0 stays low; CC = TOP gives a one-tick low
     pulse; CC > TOP stays high.
   - NFRQ toggles when the counter reaches CCx. MFRQ toggles WO[0] when the counter reaches TOP
     (CC0), the tick before the wrap, and WO[1] at each CC1 match.
   - MPWM: WO[0] is high with a one-tick low pulse from TOP to the wrap; WO[1] is NPWM on CC1.
   - STOP, the end of a one-shot and a disable all leave the pins at 0.
3. **MCx at a start**: a compare channel whose CCx equals COUNT at the start sets MCx at once
   (CC1 = 0: MC1 read set 50 us after the enable, long before the first wrap).
4. **Capture**:
   - The plain event capture (EVACT OFF) and STAMP capture on the rising edge, on the falling one
     with TCINV.
   - STAMP also captures MAX at every overflow, on every capture channel.
   - Each channel is a two-deep FIFO. Reading CCx clears MCx; if the buffer held a capture, it
     moves into CCx and MCx is set again within a couple of register accesses. With CCx and the
     buffer full, a capture sets ERR and is lost.
   - PPW: the rising edge captures the period in CC0 and restarts the count; the falling edge
     captures the width in CC1 (376 and 150 ticks for 500/200 us). PWP swaps the channels.
   - PW captures in CC0 on the falling edge, clears and stops (STATUS.STOP). It waits for the first
     active edge after the enable: with TCINV the first (inactive) edge captured nothing.
   - Absolute capture values sit one to two ticks above the twin's; differences and durations
     agree.
5. **Capture on pin** (COPEN, D2/D3): a pin muxed to the TC has its pull resistor off and floats
   (reads 0 pulled up or down) when disabled or in COPEN mode, so the pull trick cannot stimulate
   it; COPEN captured once at the enable (the floating pin) and never again. A capture channel
   without COPEN drives its pin (high in D2). Capture on pin needs a wire or the pad input path.
6. **Print timing**: a line of output takes milliseconds on silicon and nothing on the twin
   (SERCOM TX is instant there), so overflows and captures between prints differ. The checked app
   prints nothing while timing.

## 5. Open questions

- Capture on pin (COPEN) and the pin's behaviour as an input: needs an external signal or the pad
  input path (step 5 of the plan).
- CAPTMODE (CAPTMIN/CAPTMAX) exists on the JH01's TC (3.0.0), not the MC00's: not modelled.
- The function of CTRLA bit 15.
- The exact refill delay of MCx after a FIFO move (between one and two register accesses).
