# TCC (U2213) on the PIC32CM1216MC00048: waveform extension, motor control, faults, capture

Research notes for the twin, for what the first round (counting, waveform modes, commands,
buffers, events; `tccprobe.c`, `tcc.c`) left out: the waveform extension (output matrix, dead
time, swap, pattern, inversion), DSCRITICAL, the RAMP2 modes, dithering, the units each instance
has and the outputs after STOP (round B, section 4), the recoverable and non-recoverable faults
(round C, section 5), and capture and circular buffers (round D, section 6). Sources, most authoritative first: silicon (section 4), the data sheet (DS60001638E
chapter 31), the DFP headers and ATDF, the twin's code (`hw/timer/mchp_tcc.c`).

## 1. Setup on the desk board

| | |
|---|---|
| Outputs | TCC0 WO0..7 on PA04, PA05 (function E), PA18, PA19, PA22, PA23, PA16, PA17 (function F), read back through PORT.IN with PINCFG.INEN |
| Board | Bare EV15N46A. **PA18/PA19 are the DGI SPI MOSI/SCK behind the level converter**: released, they rise within 10-50 us. The other pins keep their charge when nothing drives them (stale levels at the start of a run) |
| Clock | GCLK_TCC0 1 MHz (OSC48M / 48), DIV1: a tick and a dead-time step are 1 us. tccprobe4/5 also use 48 MHz with DIV1, DIV64 and DIV1024 |
| Fault input | PA02 (EXTINT2, function A): its pull resistor, turned by PORT.OUT, makes the fault (about 2 us to cross); EIC HIGH sense, ASYNCH, event out; asynchronous EVSYS channel 0 to TCC0 MC_0 (user 11), MC_1 (12) and EV_0 (9) |
| Tools | `firmware/hello/src/scope.h` (edge recorder in RAM, SysTick over 24 bits; `scope_record_stim` also writes the stimulus at set times) |

## 2. Data sheet (31.6.2.4, 31.6.2.5, 31.6.3)

- The waveform extension takes each channel's waveform (STATUS.CMPx) through, in order, the
  output matrix (WEXCTRL.OTMX), dead-time insertion (DTIENx, DTLS, DTHS), swap (WAVE.SWAPx), the
  pattern generator (PATT, buffered by PATTBUF) and DRVCTRL.INVENx.
- OTMX: 0 WO[x] = CC(x % 4); 1 CC0 on even outputs, CC1 on odd ones; 2 CC0 on all; 3 CC0 on WO[0],
  CC1 on the rest.
- Dead time turns slice x into WO[x] (the low side) and WO[x + 4] (the high side, the inverse);
  each rises DTLS or DTHS GCLK_TCC cycles after its edge.
- DSCRITICAL: channel x set on CCx counting up and cleared on CC(x + 2) counting down. RAMP2: even
  channels in cycle A, odd ones in cycle B (STATUS.IDX); RAMP2A drives WO[0] and WO[1] from CC0;
  RAMP2C counts cycle A to CC0 and cycle B to PER.
- Dithering (CTRLA.RESOLUTION): the low 4, 5 or 6 bits of PER/CCx add a tick to that many cycles
  of every 16, 32 or 64.
- **STOP** (31.6.2.4): the counter keeps its value and "all waveforms are set to a state defined
  in DRVCTRL.NREx and DRVCTRL.NRVx"; one-shot (31.6.3.1) the same. The EV1 STOP action (pause)
  keeps the waveforms.
- **Faults** (34.6.3.5, 34.6.3.6): recoverable faults A and B from the MC0/MC1 event inputs
  (asynchronous channels), on channels 0 and 1, filtered (FILTERVAL), blanked (BLANK, BLANKVAL,
  BLANKPRESC) and qualified (QUAL); their actions clamp the channel to its inactive level (KEEP:
  to the cycle end), restart or halt the counter (HW, SW, or NR), and capture (CAPT, CAPTMIN,
  ...). Non-recoverable faults from EV0/EV1 (EVACT FAULT) put the outputs in the DRVCTRL
  NRE/NRV state.

## 4. Answers from silicon (2026-10-21)

1. **Units per instance** (B0, all ones written):

   | | WEXCTRL | DRVCTRL | PATT | WAVE |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | TCC0 (4 channels, 8 outputs) | 0xFFFF0F03 | 0xFFFFFFFF | 0xFFFF | 0x0F0F0FF7 |
   | TCC1 (2 channels, 4 outputs) | 0 | 0xFF0F0F0F | 0x0F0F | 0x000303F7 |
   | TCC2 (2 channels, 2 outputs) | 0 | 0xFF030303 | 0 | 0x000303F7 |

   The DFP's TCC parameters (OTMX, DTI, SWAP, PG) say which units an instance has. **WAVE bit 6
   sticks** (RAMP's third bit, RAMP2CS on the JH01 and E51), though the MC00's ATDF gives RAMP as
   0x30.
2. **OTMX** (B1) routes as the data sheet's table.
3. **Dead time** (B2): with DTLS 5 and DTHS 10 the low side is high CC0 - 5 ticks from 5 ticks
   after the channel rises, the high side high from 10 ticks after it falls to the wrap. Both are
   low before the first tick.
4. **SWAP** (P2) exchanges the pair's outputs, also after dead time.
5. **POL** (P1) inverts STATUS.CMPx and the pin alike.
6. **WAVE and PATT synchronize for about 4 GCLK_TCC cycles, and a write meanwhile is dropped.**
   tccprobe2 wrote WAVE twice back to back (B3, B4): the second write, with SWAP or POL, was lost,
   so they looked ineffective there. The checked app's `dropped` line pins it.
7. **PATT** (B5, P4) forces outputs after dead time; PATT reads return PATTBUF while
   STATUS.PATTBUFV is set; the update copies PATTBUF.
8. **INVEN** (B6) inverts last, after dead time.
9. **DSCRITICAL** (B7): WO[0] high from CC0 counting up to CC2 counting down, WO[1] from CC1 up to
   CC3 down; WO[2] and WO[3] as DSBOTTOM.
10. **RAMP2** (B8) as the data sheet; RAMP2A drives WO[1] from CC0 in cycle B; RAMP2C's cycle A is
    CC0 + 1 ticks with WO[0] high until CC2.
11. **Dithering** (B9, P5): PER 49 + 8/16 alternates 50- and 51-tick periods; CC0 20 + 4/16 makes
    four 21-tick pulses in 16. PER, CCx and COUNT read back with the dither bits.
12. **STOP** (S1-S4, T1-T6, tccwave `stop`):
    - COUNT reads 0 after the STOP (TOP counting down), not the value the data sheet keeps.
    - STATUS.CMPx keeps its state, except that with prescaler DIV1 it reads 0 from 5-10 GCLK_TCC
      cycles after the command (T1, T4, T5 at 1 MHz; kept with DIV2, DIV64, DIV1024).
    - **The outputs go to their non-recoverable state, as the data sheet says**: with NREx set,
      WO[x] drives NRVx (checked: WO0 low goes high, WO1 high goes low); with NREx clear the pad is
      released. Released, PA18 rose (S1, T5, T6: WO2 0 to 1 within 10-50 us; the level converter)
      while PA04 kept its charge for 200 us and more, which read at first like "kept outputs".
    - A released output with PULLEN is **pulled down whatever PORT.OUT says** (U1-U4, V1, V2),
      after a STOP and after a disable alike; without a pull it keeps its charge (V5: 1 ms). The
      release comes a few GCLK_TCC cycles after the command: at once at 48 MHz, within 10 us at
      1 MHz, 10-50 us at 192 kHz (V3, V4).
    - The end of a one-shot leaves the same levels (B10), the release at once (U5).
    - `tcc.c` had read "stopped = 1" with the pull-up on: `delay_ms(1)` can last almost nothing,
      so it read the pin before the release. It now waits 2 ms and reads 0, like the twin.
13. **Start latency**: the outputs start about 4 GCLK_TCC cycles after the enable write (4 us at
    1 MHz). The twin starts at once; checked apps measure from edges of the same run.

## 5. Faults: answers from silicon (2026-10-21)

1. **A recoverable fault needs EVCTRL.MCEIx** as well as FCTRLx.SRC (F1: SRC alone did nothing).
2. **The clamp acts on the channel, ahead of the output matrix**: fault A took WO0 and WO4 (both
   CC0 under OTMX 0) low while present; WO1 went on (F2); fault B took WO1 (F12). STATUS.CMPx
   keeps showing the waveform (G1). STATUS.FAULTxIN and FAULTx are set while the fault acts and
   clear after it; INTFLAG.FAULTx is set.
3. **KEEP** clamps to the start of the next cycle, and keeps STATUS.FAULTx set until then (F3,
   G8).
4. **QUAL**: a fault while the channel's waveform is low is ignored entirely: no FAULTxIN, no
   FAULTx, no flag (G2). Without QUAL the same fault sets them, though the output is low anyway
   (G3).
5. **RESTART** restarts the counter at the fault's rising edge (INTFLAG.TRG), without a glitch
   on the other outputs; silicon's outputs then restart about 4 cycles late, as at the enable
   (F5, G9).
6. **HALT = HW** holds the counter while the fault lasts (F6). **HALT = SW** holds it until
   software clears STATUS.FAULTx; the clamp lets go when the fault does (F7).
7. **BLANK**: RISE ignored a fault 3 us after WO0 rose (F8); FALL likewise after it fell (G12 vs
   G13); START blanks from the start of each cycle **but not the first after the enable** (G7,
   G10, G11).
8. **FILTERVAL 8**: a 3 us pulse was lost; a 15 us one acted 10-11 us after it began (F10):
   FILTERVAL plus about 2 cycles of synchronization.
9. **SRC = INVERT** makes the fault the input low (F11).
10. **CAPTURE = CAPT** captures COUNT only into a channel in capture mode (CTRLA.CPTENx): nothing
    without it (F9), CC2 = COUNT and INTFLAG.MC2 with CPTEN2 (G6).
11. **Non-recoverable** (EV0 FAULT, F13, G4; FCTRLA.HALT = NR, F14, G5): the counter stops where
    it is (not STATUS.STOP), WO0 drives NRV0, WO1 NRV1, outputs without NREx are released;
    STATUS.FAULT0 (FAULTA for HALT = NR) stays set after the input goes. Software clearing it
    resumes counting from where it stopped, the waveform back on the pins, after a few cycles.
12. **STATUS reads lag** the fault by 5-8 GCLK_TCC cycles at 1 MHz (G1-G3): read STATUS well after
    a change, or watch the pins.

## 6. Capture and circular buffers: answers from silicon (2026-10-21)

1. **Event capture** (CTRLA.CPTENx with EVCTRL.MCEIx, D1-D3): COUNT on the MCx event's rising
   edge. Each channel is a FIFO: CCx (INTFLAG.MCx), then CCBUFx (STATUS.CCBUFVx); a third capture
   sets INTFLAG.ERR and is lost. Reading CCx clears MCx and moves CCBUFx up a couple of GCLK_TCC
   cycles later (back-to-back reads see the old value, E1), MCx set again. Without MCEIx nothing
   is captured.
2. **PPW and PWP** (EVACT1 with TCEI1, E1-E5) follow the data sheet: PPW captures the period into
   CC0 and restarts the counter (INTFLAG.TRG) on the rising edge, the pulse width into CC1 on the
   falling one; PWP swaps the channels; TCINV1 swaps the edges. The first period capture is the
   time from the enable to the first rising edge. Only CPTENx channels capture (E5).
3. **Dual slope** with TOP below MAX/2: a capture's MSB (bit 23) is set on the down ramp (D7).
4. **Buffers at the enable**: a CCBUFx or PERBUF written before the enable is copied at the first
   update condition, not at the enable (H6). A PER or CCx write also writes its buffer: PERBUF and
   CCBUFx read back the same value.
5. **Circular buffers** (WAVE.CIPEREN, CICCENx; H1-H5): each update swaps the register and its
   buffer. Single slope: every cycle (CC0 29 and 10 take turns, H5); RAMP2: cycle A takes one
   value and B the other (H3, H4); DSBOTH: CCx swaps at TOP and ZERO (10 counting up, 30 counting
   down, H1), PER at ZERO only (a whole cycle of 49, then one of 29, H2). The valid flag clears
   at the first update in single slope, at the first update that starts cycle A (ZERO) in RAMP2
   and DSBOTH. PER and CCx reads show the buffer while it is valid or circular.
6. **STATUS.FAULTnIN** shows an enabled EVn input's level whatever its event action (0x800 with
   TCINV1 and the input low, E3).

## 7. The twin

`hw/timer/mchp_tcc.c` models all of sections 4 to 6 except the start and restart latency, the
STATUS read lag and the floating pads' levels (a floating pad reads 0 on the twin). STOP and one-shot put each output at NRVx or release
it. The units an instance lacks come from `MchpInstanceInfo.features` and `.outputs`, which the
generator takes from the DFP's TCC parameters.

The dithering qtest found a timing bug shared with the TC: the counter's base time took the
timer's "+1 ns" with every event, so the counter lost a tick every ~250 events at 4 MHz (and every
~20 at 48 MHz). Both models now keep tick instants exact (t_base plus a 2^-32 ns fraction) and
round only the timer deadline up; `pic32cm-tc/long-run` and `pic32cm-tcc/dither` cover it.

## 8. Open questions

- What RAMP2CS (WAVE.RAMP = 4) does on the MC00: the bit sticks, the data sheet has no mode.
- Why STATUS.CMPx clears after a STOP with DIV1 only.
- The EV1 STOP action (pause) keeping the outputs is the data sheet's word, not yet probed.
- Faults, not probed: the ALTFAULT source, BLANKPRESC, the capture modes other than CAPT, a
  clamp with inverted polarity (WAVE.POLx: "inactive" may then be high), RAMP2's per-cycle
  faults, the debug fault (DBGCTRL.FDDBD) and the update fault (UFS).
- Capture: the EVACT0 STAMP action, the fault capture modes but CAPT, STATUS.CMPx of a capture
  channel (silicon showed it set and clear without a clear rule), the exact FIFO refill delay.
- CTRLBSET.IDXCMD; circular buffers with DMA.
