# ADC (U2247) on the PIC32CM1216MC00048: timing, sequences, events, DMA and the client ADC

Research notes for the twin, for what the first ADC round (`adc.c`: references, inputs, INTREF and
VREFOE, the correction, the window) left out: conversion time, free running, automatic sequences,
the START and SYNC (flush) event inputs, the event outputs, the RESRDY DMA request, double
buffering and ADC1 as ADC0's client. Sources, most authoritative first: silicon (section 4), the
data sheet (DS60001638E chapter 36), the DFP, the twin's code (`hw/adc/mchp_adc_u2247.c`).

## 1. Setup on the desk board

| | |
|---|---|
| Inputs | SCALEDVDDCORE (0x1A) and SCALEDVDDANA (0x1B): no pins. The desk board runs VDDANA at 5 V (4.89 V), the twin at 3.3 V, so checks compare values as orders |
| Clock | GCLK0 (48 MHz) to ADC0 and ADC1; DIV32 makes CLK_ADC 1.5 MHz, 32 CPU cycles. Times are CPU cycles from SysTick |
| Event input | PA02 (EXTINT2) with its pull turned by PORT.OUT, HIGH sense, ASYNCH, asynchronous EVSYS channel 0 to ADC0 START (user 28) or SYNC (user 29, the flush) |
| DMA | DMAC channel 0, TRIGSRC 36 (0x24), beat-triggered halfwords from RESULT |

## 2. Data sheet (36.6.2.8, 36.6.2.12, 36.6.3, 36.6.6)

- f_CLK_ADC = f_GCLK_ADC / 2^(1 + CTRLB.PRESCALER); in free running RS = f_CLK_ADC / (n_SAMPLING +
  n_OFFCOMP + n_DATA); the propagation delay is (1 + resolution) / f_CLK_ADC. With OFFCOMP the
  sampling time is 4 cycles.
- Sequences convert the SEQCTRL inputs from the lowest; SEQSTATUS.SEQBUSY while running, SEQSTATE
  the last input converted.
- Events: START and FLUSH inputs (asynchronous paths only, rising edge unless xINV), RESRDY and
  WINMON outputs. FLUSH aborts conversions in progress; "after the flush, the ADC will resume where
  it left off".
- Host/client: ADC1.CTRLA.SLAVEEN routes GCLK_ADC0 and ADC0's controls to ADC1; DUALSEL BOTH or
  INTERLEAVE.
- INPUTCTRL, CTRLC, AVGCTRL, SAMPCTRL, WINLT, WINUT, GAINCORR and OFFSETCORR are double buffered.

## 4. Answers from silicon (2026-10-21)

1. **Conversion time**: N x (SAMPLEN + 1, or 4 with OFFCOMP, + the resolution's bits) + 1 CLK_ADC
   cycles, N the averaged samples (16-bit mode), plus about 36 GCLK cycles for the start. 12 bits,
   SAMPLEN 0: 14 CLK_ADC + 36 (486 cycles at DIV32, 3620 at DIV256, 148 at DIV8, R1-R3); SAMPLEN
   15 adds 15 CLK_ADC, OFFCOMP 3, 8 bits takes 4 fewer; 16 samples take 16 x 13 + 1 (R1-R8).
2. **Start-up**: no conversion starts within about 10 us of the enable. A conversion started at
   once waits (+480 cycles at every prescaler, R1-R3); one started 50 us later does not (E1).
3. **SWTRIG.START** reads 1 until the conversion starts (1-2 CLK_ADC after the write).
4. **Free running** converts back to back every N x (sampling + bits) CLK_ADC (13 at 12 bits),
   starting at the enable with FREERUN set; a START then adds nothing (adcprobe5 Q1-Q3).
5. **Sequences**: the SEQCTRL inputs from the lowest, RESRDY each; SEQSTATUS SEQBUSY | input while
   more follow, the last input alone at the end (0x9A then 0x1B). Unread, the last result stays with
   OVERRUN (S2). With FREERUN the sequence repeats and SEQBUSY stays set (S3).
6. **Events**: START starts on the rising edge, the falling one with STARTINV (E1, E2). SYNC (the
   flush) aborts the conversion in progress; free running, the next starts at once (E3: one start
   and conversion after the flush). RESRDYEO reaches an EVSYS channel (E4). Detaching the event user
   with STARTINV set makes an edge of its own (the line goes to 0).
7. **DMA**: TRIGSRC **36** (0x24) is ADC0 RESRDY, as the ATDF's `DMAC_ID_RESRDY` says; dmac.h's
   `DMAC_CHCTRLB_TRIGSRC_ADC0_RESRDY` (0x2A) never fires (adcprobe4; the DMAC note had found the
   value-group wrong from 0x0A up). A free-running sequence moves one result a beat, in order. A
   result left unread when a channel is enabled moves at once.
8. **ADC1 as client** (needs GCLK_ADC1 on for its own SWRST): BOTH converts both on one ADC0
   START; INTERLEAVE alternates ADC0, ADC1. ADC1's CTRLA reads SLAVEEN only.
9. **Double buffering**: an INPUTCTRL write right after a free-running result applied to the very
   next result (B1).
10. **Out of range**: DIV2 at 48 MHz (24 MHz CLK_ADC, above the 16 MHz limit) reads 0.

## 5. The twin

`hw/adc/mchp_adc_u2247.c` converts on a timer with the times of section 4 and models free running,
sequences, the events, the RESRDY DMA request (a level trigger on INTFLAG that the ADC kicks the
DMAC for), and the client. Registers act at once. Not modelled: the analog clock limit; the
DMAC's reaction to a request already set at a channel's enable comes at the next DMAC register
read or 20 us later, not at once (`mchp_dmac_u2223.c` batches transfers).

## 6. Open questions

- The exact start latency (36 GCLK cycles fits within the polling step) and whether the 10 us
  start-up is fixed time or GCLK cycles (GCLK_ADC was the CPU clock throughout).
- Double buffering for a conversion already sampling: the twin applies every write at once.
- Sleep: RUNSTDBY, ONDEMAND (step 4 of the plan).
